Engineering

Recruit at least one Engineer, open the build menu (Tab, the BUILD button, or L3 on a controller), pick a structure and place it. Plastic is paid when you place the blueprint; the nearest free Engineer walks over and builds it.

KeyStructureCostBuild timeWhat it does
1Sandbag wall253 sBlocks movement and bullets; faces the direction you placed it from
2Pillbox1208 sGreen bunker with a machine gun that defends on its own
3Minefield604 sSeven mines in a ring; they arm after a second
4Bridge506 sSpans a river so ground troops and vehicles can cross (tan troops use it too)

Rivers run through Operation Backyard, Mountain Pass and Tundra Push. Each has two shallow fords; bridges add more crossings. Shots, planes and choppers pass over water.

Field tips

  1. Bunkers keep producing tan troops until destroyed. Bazookas, tank shells and grenades bring them down fastest.
  2. On big maps, push from outpost to outpost: recruits and Sarge's respawns come from the outpost nearest to him.
  3. Sarge slowly heals when he hasn't been hit for a few seconds. Medics heal everyone, and repair vehicles too.
  4. Vehicles flatten tan infantry. Choppers ignore walls but can be shot down.
  5. In the final mission the bunkers power the Overlord's shield. Destroy them first.
